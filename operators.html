Meet the Operators

Delta Force: Hawk Ops features a roster of unique operators, each with a distinct tactical ability that changes how your team approaches every fight. Whether you're the aggressive breacher, the aerial scout, or the squad medic, picking the right operator — and knowing when to use their ability — can turn the tide of any match.

Below you'll find our current tier list, plus in-depth guides for each operator covering ability usage, team synergies, weapon pairings, and mode-specific strategies for both Havoc Operations and All-Out Warfare.

Operator Tier List — Season 7/8

Current meta rankings based on versatility, impact in both modes, and team utility. Updated for the latest balance patch.

T0

Red Wolf

Aggressive Breacher

Smoke screen + slide king. Dominates close-quarters combat and forces enemy rotations. The most impactful operator in both modes when played aggressively.

Weilong

Aerial Assault

Jetpack vertical flanks. Unmatched map control and surprise factor. High skill ceiling with insane payoff when mastered.

T1

Silver Wing

Recon Intel

Recon drones reveal enemy positions for the entire team. Best operator for coordinated squads and information warfare. Less impact solo.

Hive Medic

Support Healer

Healing smoke keeps squads alive in extended fights. Essential for Havoc extraction and clutch All-Out holds. Weak alone, unstoppable with a team.

How we rank: T0 operators shape the meta and perform well in most situations. T1 operators are strong picks that excel in specific team comps or modes. Tier rankings are updated with each balance patch.

Team Composition Tips

⚔️

Balanced Squad

For Havoc trios: run Red Wolf + Hive Medic + Silver Wing for breach power, sustain, and intel coverage all in one squad.

🛡️

Aggressive Push

For All-Out Warfare: Red Wolf + Weilong creates a pincer with smoke cover from above and below. Devastating on objective pushes.

🧠

Solo Play

Playing solo? Red Wolf and Weilong scale best alone. Hive Medic needs a team to be effective — avoid as a solo pick.
