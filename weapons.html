Weapon Tips & Tricks

🔧

Copy Modification Codes

Don't reinvent the wheel. Our budget builds include copy-paste modification codes you can import directly in-game. Test them, tweak them, make them yours.

🎯

Match Range to Role

Running Havoc on Dam Zero? You'll want mid-range ARs. Longbow Valley? Bring a DMR. Knowing your map's engagement distances saves you from getting outranged.

💰

Prioritize Recoil Control

For new players, a low-recoil build beats raw damage every time. You can't deal damage if you can't hit. Start with grip + compensator, then add damage attachments later.

⚡

Carry a Sidearm

Running a sniper or DMR? Always carry a pistol or SMG for close-range surprises. Getting caught at 5 meters with a QJB201 is a death sentence.
