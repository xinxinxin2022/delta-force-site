Delta Force Game Modes Overview

Delta Force: Hawk Ops offers two core experiences that couldn't be more different. Havoc Operations is the tense, loot-driven extraction mode where every decision could be your last. All-Out Warfare is a massive 32v32 battlefield with vehicles, armor, and objective-based chaos. Choose your battlefield — or master both.

Whether you're sneaking through Dam Zero with a backpack full of gold or calling in armor support on a 64-player frontline, understanding the rules, pacing, and economy of each mode is the difference between winning and going home empty-handed.

Havoc Operations vs All-Out Warfare

Not sure which mode to jump into? Here's a side-by-side breakdown of what each offers.

Feature Havoc Operations All-Out Warfare
Game Type Extraction / Survival Large-scale PvP (32v32)
Team Size Solo / Duo / Trio 32 players per team
Match Length 20–40 minutes 15–25 minutes
Core Objective Loot, survive, extract Capture points, destroy targets
Lose Gear on Death? Yes (if not extracted) No
Economy Havoc Coins — persistent Loadout-based, no loss
Vehicles Limited (ATVs, boats) Tanks, helicopters, armor
Maps Dam Zero, Longbow Valley, Bakhsh, Aerospace Base Dedicated large-scale battlefields
Pacing Slow, tense, methodical Fast, chaotic, team-driven
Best For Solo players, loot hunters, risk-takers Team players, objective-focused, vehicle fans

Quick Tips for Both Modes

🎯

Start with All-Out Warfare

New to Delta Force? Learn gunplay and map awareness in All-Out Warfare where dying has no penalty. Then graduate to Havoc when you're comfortable.

💰

Run Knife Runs in Havoc

Broke? Bring only a knife, sprint to high-value loot spots, grab what you can, and extract. Repeat until you can afford a proper loadout.

🚁

Vehicles Win Games

In All-Out Warfare, armor and helicopters decide the match. Learn to call them in, use them for flanking, and counter enemy vehicles with AA support.
