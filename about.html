About Us

The community behind the most comprehensive Delta Force: Hawk Ops English-language guide.

Who We Are

We are a passionate community of experienced Delta Force: Hawk Ops players who have dedicated hundreds of hours to mastering every aspect of the game. Our team includes former competitive FPS players, content creators, and dedicated grinders who have been following the Delta Force franchise since its early days.

What started as a small Discord group sharing tips and strategies has grown into a comprehensive resource used by thousands of players worldwide. We believe that great games deserve great community support, and we're committed to making Hawk Ops accessible to everyone — from complete beginners to seasoned veterans looking to refine their edge.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: provide the most comprehensive, accurate, and up-to-date English-language Delta Force: Hawk Ops guide on the internet. We aim to be the first stop for any player looking to improve their game, whether they're struggling with basic mechanics or pushing for the top ranks.

We believe that knowledge should be free and accessible. That's why every guide we publish is thoroughly tested, community-reviewed, and updated whenever the game changes. No paywalls, no half-finished information — just solid, actionable strategies that work.

What We Cover

Our guides span every major aspect of Delta Force: Hawk Ops:

  • Game Modes — In-depth strategy guides for Havoc Operations (extraction mode) and All-Out Warfare (32v32 large-scale battles), including spawn points, objectives, vehicle tactics, and team coordination.
  • Operators — Detailed breakdowns of every operator's abilities, playstyles, synergies, and counters. From Red Wolf's aggressive breach tactics to Silver Wing's surveillance expertise, we cover them all.
  • Weapons — Current meta tier lists, budget builds for new players, advanced modification codes, recoil pattern analysis, and attachment recommendations for every situation.
  • Maps — Complete map guides with loot locations, knife run routes, extraction points, sniper positions, and high-value target zones for Dam Zero, Longbow Valley, Bakhsh Aerospace Base, and more.
  • Economy — The complete breakdown of Havoc Coins, money-making strategies, loot valuation, and how to fund your loadouts without grinding endlessly.
  • Beginner Resources — Essential tips, common mistakes to avoid, movement mechanics, positioning fundamentals, and everything new players need to get started on the right foot.

Our Commitment

We update our guides regularly to reflect:

  • Seasonal updates and balance patches
  • New operator releases and reworks
  • Weapon stat changes and new meta developments
  • Map rotations and new environment additions
  • Community-discovered strategies and techniques

If something in our guides is outdated or incorrect, we want to know. We actively monitor feedback and work with the community to ensure our information is always current.
