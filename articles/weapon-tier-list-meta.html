Delta Force Weapon Tier List 2026 — Best Guns & Meta Rankings (Havoc & All-Out Warfare)

Delta Force Guide Team Updated July 24, 2026 12 min read

The weapon meta in Delta Force: Hawk Ops shifts with every balance patch, but as of July 2026 a clear hierarchy has formed in both Havoc extraction and All-Out Warfare ranked. Some weapons are dominant in both modes, some only in one, and a few — like the M7 — refuse to leave the top slot no matter what the devs do.

This guide gives you the full tier list, with reasoning for every placement, plus notes on which weapons are rising, which are falling, and which are budget-friendly traps you should avoid until you can afford the real meta picks.

Havoc Weapon Tier List

Havoc is the extraction mode — longer matches, higher stakes, and an economy that rewards reliable weapons you can actually afford to lose. In Havoc, consistency beats peak DPS, and weapons with good availability and manageable recoil dominate the top tiers.

TierWeaponTypeWhy It Ranks Here
T0 M7 Assault Rifle Best all-rounder. Manageable recoil, strong damage at all ranges, plentiful ammo. The default pick for a reason.
T0.5 AKM Assault Rifle Higher per-bullet damage than the M7, but rougher recoil. Rewards controlled bursts. A budget-friendly beast.
T0.5 MK47 Assault Rifle Burst/precision rifle with excellent damage. Demands good aim; punishes spray hard. Elite players put it at T0.
T0.5 MP7 SMG Top-tier SMG for close-quarters. Fast TTK, good mobility. Dominates indoor loot zones like Admin Center.
T1 QJB201 LMG The budget LMG king. Cheap, reliable, huge magazine. Loses to the M7 in DPS but wins on cost-efficiency.
T1 CAR-15 Assault Rifle Very fast handling, strong in close-to-mid range. Great budget option that punches above its cost.
T1 M16A4 Assault Rifle Burst-fire classic. Excellent at mid-long range, weak in close-quarters spray. High skill ceiling.
T2 AKS74 Assault Rifle Outclassed by the AKM and M7. Only worth running if that is what you can afford early in the economy.
T2 SG552 Assault Rifle Niche pick with awkward damage breakpoints. Works in specific builds, but rarely a first choice.
📌 Budget Note

If you are losing gear often early in your Havoc career, start with the QJB201 at ~60K and the CAR-15 as your sidearm. The loss economy matters more than the DPS gap at low economy levels.

All-Out Warfare Weapon Tier List

All-Out Warfare is pure PvP — faster TTK expectations, different pacing, and a much stronger premium for weapons that win the first shot. Here, the meta shifts slightly: SMGs rise, shotguns become viable, and the assault-rifle hierarchy tightens.

TierWeaponTypeWhy It Ranks Here
T0 M7 Assault Rifle Still the king. Versatile, consistent, works at every range. The safe first-pick in loadout select.
T1 AK-12 Assault Rifle Hard recoil but elite DPS. Wins fights at mid-range if you can control it. Meta pick for aggressive players.
T1 CAR-15 Assault Rifle Fast and forgiving. Dominates close-to-mid range. Excellent for aggressive flanking playstyles.
T1 P90 SMG 50-round magazine plus great mobility. The SMG to beat in All-Out Warfare for close-range fights.
T1.5 M870 Shotgun Situational but powerful. Dominates indoor objective holds; useless at range. Pick it for specific maps.
⚠️ Shotgun Warning

Do not bring the M870 to an open map like open-field rotations. It is a specialist tool — bring it when you know the fight will be within 15 meters, not as a default loadout.

Why These Weapons? The Meta Logic

The M7 problem

The M7 is the only weapon that is T0 in both modes, and it has been since early access. The reason is simple: it has no bad range, no bad situation, and no major weakness. It does not have the highest DPS, the fastest TTK, or the largest magazine — but it is good enough at everything that it is almost never the wrong pick. Until the devs nerf its consistency, it will stay at the top.

AKM and MK47: the Havoc duopoly

These two sit just below the M7 in Havoc because they both reward skill more than the M7 does. The AKM hits harder per bullet but kicks harder — you need to commit to controlled bursts. The MK47 is even more demanding: its burst fire punishes panic-spraying, but in the hands of a precise player it deletes enemies faster than anything else in its class.

SMGs: MP7 and P90

SMGs rise in All-Out Warfare because the mode's pacing favors close-quarters fights. The MP7 is the Havoc SMG of choice because it also performs respectably at mid-range when you get caught out. The P90 is pure close-quarters dominance, with a magazine that lets you fight through multiple enemies without reloading.

Budget vs. meta

Some of the most important weapons in the game are not the most expensive. The CAR-15 is cheap and T1 in both modes. The QJB201 can cost as little as 60K and still hang with premium rifles. Smart players build around the economy, not around the tier list alone — see our Budget Weapon Builds guide for full details.

Rising and Falling

  • Rising: P90 — recent QoL buffs have made it the best close-quarters option in All-Out Warfare.
  • Rising: QJB201 — as the Havoc economy gets tighter, budget LMGs become more attractive.
  • Falling: AKS74 — every relevant stat is outclassed by the AKM and M7.
  • Stable: M7 — the one constant in every patch. Do not expect it to drop any time soon.
✅ TL;DR

Pick the M7 if you want one weapon for both modes. Pick the AKM or MK47 if you want Havoc dominance. Pick the P90 or AK-12 for All-Out Warfare. Pick the QJB201 or CAR-15 when your wallet is thin.
