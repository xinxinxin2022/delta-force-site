General Questions

1 What is Delta Force: Hawk Ops? +
Delta Force: Hawk Ops is a free-to-play tactical first-person shooter developed by Team Jade and published by Garena. It features multiple game modes including Havoc Operations (extraction/PvE), All-Out Warfare (32v32 large-scale battles), and supports both solo and squad gameplay across diverse maps and environments.
2 Is Delta Force: Hawk Ops free to play? +
Yes, Delta Force: Hawk Ops is completely free to play. The game follows a free-to-play model with optional cosmetic purchases and battle passes. All gameplay-affecting content, including operators, weapons, and maps, can be earned through gameplay.
3 What platforms is the game available on? +
Delta Force: Hawk Ops is available on PC (Windows) and mobile platforms (iOS and Android). The game features cross-platform play in some modes. Performance and controls vary between platforms, with PC offering the most precise controls and mobile providing on-the-go gaming.
4 How often does the game update? +
Delta Force: Hawk Ops receives regular updates, typically including seasonal content with new operators, weapons, maps, and game modes. Major balance patches happen between seasons. We update our guides after each significant patch to reflect changes in the meta, operator abilities, and weapon performance.

Game Modes

5 What are the main game modes? +
Delta Force: Hawk Ops has two primary game modes: Havoc Operations, an extraction-based PvE/PvP mode where you loot, fight AI enemies, and extract with valuable items; and All-Out Warfare, a 32v32 large-scale team battle featuring vehicles, armor, helicopters, and objective-based combat. For detailed breakdowns, see our Game Modes page.
6 What is Havoc Operations? +
Havoc Operations is an extraction mode similar to Escape from Tarkov. You enter a map as a solo player or squad, fight AI-controlled enemies and other players, loot valuable items, and must reach an extraction point to keep your earnings. If you die, you lose everything you brought in and looted. Read our complete Havoc Operations Guide for strategies.
7 How do vehicles work in All-Out Warfare? +
Vehicles in All-Out Warfare include armored vehicles, transport trucks, helicopters, and boats. You need to find and enter them at spawn points. Vehicles have health bars and can be destroyed by concentrated fire or anti-vehicle weapons. Helicopters provide aerial reconnaissance and fire support but are vulnerable to anti-air weapons.
8 Can I play solo or do I need a team? +
Delta Force: Hawk Ops supports both solo and squad play. In Havoc Operations, you can queue solo but will face squads. In All-Out Warfare, you're always in a large team. While you can play solo, communication and teamwork with a squad significantly improves your success rate, especially in extraction mode.

Operators

9 Who is the best operator for beginners? +
For beginners, we recommend starting with Hive Medic (support/healing) or Silver Wing (recon/intel). Hive Medic's healing smoke can keep you alive while you learn, and Silver Wing's recon drones give you information to avoid danger. Both operators are forgiving for players still learning game mechanics. See our Operators page for full details.
10 What is Red Wolf's playstyle? +
Red Wolf is an aggressive close-quarters specialist known as the "Slide King." His abilities include smoke screens and enhanced sliding mechanics that allow him to breach enemy positions and outmaneuver opponents in tight spaces. He excels in aggressive pushes and flanking attacks but requires good movement skills to use effectively. Full details in our Red Wolf Guide.
11 How does Weilong's jetpack work? +
Weilong's jetpack ability allows him to launch into the air for aerial assaults and tactical repositioning. You can use it to surprise enemies from above, reach elevated positions quickly, or escape dangerous situations. It has a cooldown period, so timing your jumps is crucial for maximum effectiveness. Read more in our Weilong Guide.
12 What does Silver Wing's recon drone do? +
Silver Wing can deploy a recon drone that reveals enemy positions within a radius. This intel is shared with your entire squad and appears on the mini-map. It's invaluable for planning pushes, avoiding ambushes, and gathering information before engaging. The drone can be shot down by enemies, so positioning matters.

Weapons & Economy

13 What are the best weapons in the current meta? +
The weapon meta changes with updates and patches. Generally, assault rifles are solid all-around choices, SMGs excel in close quarters, and LMGs provide strong sustained fire. Check our Weapon Tier List for the most up-to-date rankings based on current game balance.
14 How do weapon modification codes work? +
Weapon modification codes are shareable loadout strings that let you copy other players' weapon builds exactly. You can enter a code in the weapon customization screen to automatically apply all attachments and modifications. This saves time and ensures you get the optimal setup. See our Budget Weapon Builds for codes you can copy.
15 How do I earn Havoc Coins fast? +
The fastest ways to earn Havoc Coins include: running knife fights in the first few minutes of a match (high risk, high reward), flipping mid-tier loot items on the market, completing Havoc missions, and farming specific high-value loot spawns on maps like Dam Zero. See our Economy Guide for detailed strategies.
16 What is the economy system in Havoc? +
The economy system revolves around Havoc Coins, the in-game currency. You earn coins by extracting loot, completing missions, and selling items on the market. You spend coins on weapons, attachments, armor, and supplies before each raid. Managing your economy is crucial — going into debt limits your options, while smart investing can give you better gear.

Maps & Strategy

17 Which map is best for farming money? +
Dam Zero is generally considered the best map for beginners to farm money due to its straightforward layout and good loot density. Bakhsh Aerospace Base has higher-value loot but also more dangerous encounters. Longbow Valley offers good mid-tier value with varied terrain. See our individual map guides for specific loot routes.
18 How do I extract successfully in Havoc Operations? +
To extract successfully: know your extraction point locations before you go in, plan your route early (don't wait until the last minute), avoid unnecessary fights once you have valuable loot, use smoke for cover when moving to extraction, and be aware that extraction points often attract other players camping the area.
19 What should I do if I keep dying early? +
If you're dying early in Havoc Operations: start by playing conservatively, avoid hot-drop zones, use side routes to reach loot areas, listen for audio cues (gunfire, footsteps), always have an escape plan, and don't engage every enemy you see. Focus on learning the map before trying to clear it. Check our Beginner Tips article for more advice.
20 How do I level up quickly? +
Level up quickly by completing daily missions and challenges, focusing on objective-based play rather than just kills, playing regularly to accumulate experience, and participating in special events that offer bonus XP. Havoc Operations tends to yield more XP per match if you survive and extract successfully.
