Contributing to the Guide

We welcome contributions from the community! If you have deep expertise in a particular operator, map, or strategy and want to help improve our guides, please reach out via the form above with the subject "Want to Contribute."

We look for contributors who:

  • Have significant gameplay experience (especially ranked/extraction mode)
  • Can provide data-backed claims (screenshots, video evidence, stat comparisons)
  • Write clearly and can explain complex concepts simply
  • Are willing to have their work reviewed and edited for consistency
