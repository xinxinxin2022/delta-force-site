## When to Upgrade

Don't upgrade just because you can afford it. Here's when to move from budget to meta:

- **Under 500K bankroll:** Stay on budget weapons. Every death costs less.
- **500K–1M bankroll:** You can afford the MK47 budget build or AKM consistently.
- **1M–3M bankroll:** Time to try the M7 or AS VAL. You can absorb occasional losses.
- **3M+ bankroll:** Full meta loadouts. You're playing to win, not to survive.

<Callout type="success" title="The Budget Player's Advantage">
Budget players have a hidden advantage: **they're not afraid to lose their gear**. This makes them more aggressive, more willing to take fights, and ultimately more likely to improve their skills. The player who brings a 60K QJB201 into every match will learn faster than the player who brings a 600K M7 and plays scared.
</Callout>


## FAQ

### What is the cheapest viable weapon build in Delta Force?
The **QJB201 no-recoil build at ~60K** is the cheapest viable build. It has near-zero recoil, a 40-round magazine, and performs well in both Havoc and All-Out Warfare.

### Is the MK47 worth building on a budget?
Absolutely. The viral 70K MK47 build delivers T0.5-level damage for a fraction of the cost. The key is the Gen 2 resonance grip, which solves the MK47's historical recoil problems. However, it demands good aim — the burst fire punishes spray-and-pray.

### Should beginners use budget weapons?
Yes, 100%. Budget weapons let you learn the game without the constant stress of losing expensive gear. Start with the QJB201 at 60K, upgrade to the AKM at 200K, and only move to meta weapons once you have a 1M+ bankroll.

### How much should I spend per Havoc run?
A good rule of thumb: **never spend more than 20% of your bankroll on a single loadout**. If you have 500K, your max loadout should be 100K. If you have 100K, stick to 20K budget builds.

### Are modification codes shared in the game?
Yes. Delta Force lets players share weapon build codes. Find working builds online (Bilibili, YouTube, Reddit) and copy the code directly into your weapon mod screen. No need to figure out combinations yourself.

### What's the best budget weapon for All-Out Warfare?
The **CAR-15 speed build (~150K)** is the best budget option for AOW. Its fast handling and strong close-to-mid range performance match the mode's pacing perfectly.
