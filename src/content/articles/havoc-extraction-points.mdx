## Dam Zero Extraction Points

Dam Zero has four main extraction points, each with unique characteristics and challenges.

### 1. North Bridge Extraction

**Location**: Northern edge of map, bridge crossing
**Activation Time**: 10 seconds
**Spawn Rate**: Always available
**Risk Level**: Medium

<Screenshot src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/guoyx/delta-force-site@images-v1/images/damzero-north-bridge-extraction.jpg" alt="Dam Zero North Bridge extraction point" />

**Safe Approach Strategy**:

- Approach from the south using building cover
- Check the bridge approaches before committing
- Use the destroyed vehicles on the bridge for cover during activation
- Have an escape route planned back into the city

**Common Ambush Spots**:

- Rooftops overlooking the bridge approaches
- Behind the destroyed vehicles on the bridge itself
- In the buildings across the bridge

**Timing Windows**:

- **Early game (0-3 min)**: Low risk, few teams have reached this area
- **Mid game (3-7 min)**: Medium risk, teams rotating through
- **Late game (7+ min)**: High risk, remaining teams camping extractions

<Callout type="tip" title="North Bridge Pro Tip">
The North Bridge extraction has two spawn locations in Season 8, making it harder for campers to control both. Check both spawn points before committing to one.
</Callout>

### 2. South Road Extraction

**Location**: Southern edge of map, road intersection
**Activation Time**: 10 seconds
**Spawn Rate**: Increased 25% in Season 8
**Risk Level**: Medium

**Safe Approach Strategy**:

- Use the buildings along the south road for cover
- Approach from the east or west to avoid the main road
- Check the intersection before committing
- Use the new hard cover added in Season 8

**Common Ambush Spots**:

- Buildings overlooking the intersection
- Behind the new hard cover (can work both ways)
- Along the road approaches

**Timing Windows**:

- **Early game**: Very low risk
- **Mid game**: Low-medium risk
- **Late game**: Medium-high risk

### 3. East Road Extraction

**Location**: Eastern edge of map, road leading out
**Activation Time**: 10 seconds
**Spawn Rate**: Standard
**Risk Level**: Medium-High

**Safe Approach Strategy**:

- Use the Military Compound buildings for cover during approach
- Check the road before committing
- Use the tree line for cover if available
- Have alternative routes planned

**Common Ambush Spots**:

- Military Compound rooftops
- Along the road itself
- In the tree line

**Timing Windows**:

- **Early game**: Low risk
- **Mid game**: Medium risk (teams rotating from Military Compound)
- **Late game**: High risk

### 4. Underground Tunnel Extraction

**Location**: Central-south area, tunnel entrance
**Activation Time**: 20 seconds (increased from 15 in Season 8)
**Spawn Rate**: Increased spawn rate
**Risk Level**: Low-Medium

**Safe Approach Strategy**:

- Enter the tunnel from multiple angles if in a squad
- Clear the tunnel before activating extraction
- Use the tunnel walls for cover during activation
- Listen for enemies approaching from other tunnel entrances

**Common Ambush Spots**:

- Other tunnel entrances
- Inside the tunnel itself (less common)
- Outside the main entrance

**Timing Windows**:

- **All game phases**: Consistently low-medium risk

<Callout type="warning" title="Tunnel Extraction Timing">
The Season 8 change increased activation time from 15 to 20 seconds. This makes you more vulnerable during extraction. Clear the tunnel thoroughly before committing.
</Callout>

## Anti-Camper Strategies

Extraction campers are a constant threat in Havoc Operations. Here are proven strategies to avoid and counter them:

### Detection Strategies

**Use Luna's Recon Drone**: If your squad has Luna, use the drone to scout extraction points before committing. This reveals enemy positions and allows you to plan accordingly.

**Listen Carefully**: Extraction campers often make noise — footsteps, weapon handling, voice chat. Listen for these audio cues before approaching.

**Check Multiple Angles**: Campers often position themselves to cover the most obvious approach routes. Look for alternative angles and check them before committing.

**Watch for Movement**: Look for movement in windows, on rooftops, and behind cover. Campers often reposition, giving away their location.

### Avoidance Strategies

**Use Less Popular Extractions**: The Underground Tunnel, Side Alley, and Emergency Exit are less commonly camped than Main Gate or North Bridge.

**Time Your Extraction**: Early game extractions (0-3 minutes) have lower camper density. Late game extractions (8+ minutes) are riskier as remaining teams set up camp.

**Approach from Unexpected Angles**: Don't always use the most direct route. Approach from flanking angles or alternative routes to avoid camper sight lines.

**Coordinate with Squad**: If playing in a squad, have one player scout while others wait. Communicate enemy positions before committing.

### Counter Strategies

**Smoke Grenades**: Use smoke to obscure your approach and activation. This forces campers to either hold their fire or reveal their position.

**Flash Grenades**: If you suspect a camper, throw a flash grenade to stun them before committing to the extraction.

**Aggressive Push**: If you know a camper's position and have the advantage, push them aggressively before they can react.

**Alternative Extraction**: If an extraction point is clearly camped, don't waste time fighting. Move to an alternative extraction point.

## Squad Extraction Tactics

Extracting with a squad requires coordination and communication. Here are proven squad extraction tactics:

### The Scout and Wait

One player scouts the extraction point while others wait in cover. The scout reports enemy positions, and the squad commits only when the area is clear.

### The Cover and Extract

Two players provide covering fire while two players activate the extraction. Once the first two are extracted, the covering players extract.

### The Decoy

One player acts as a decoy, drawing camper attention while the rest of the squad extracts from a different angle.

### The Sweep

The squad sweeps the extraction area together, clearing all potential camper positions before activating the extraction.
