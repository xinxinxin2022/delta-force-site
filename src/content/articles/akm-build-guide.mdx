## AKM Recoil Pattern & Mastery

### The AKM Recoil Pattern

The AKM has a **distinctive recoil pattern** that differs from other ARs:

```
Shots 1-3:   Sharp vertical kick (pull down hard)
Shots 4-8:   Vertical + strong right drift (pull down-left)
Shots 9-15:  Stabilizes but still climbs (controlled bursts)
Shots 16-30: Unmanageable without meta build (avoid)
```

### Control Techniques

**1. The Two-Tap**
- Fire 2 rounds, pause, fire 2 more
- Most accurate at 40-50m
- Requires discipline but devastating TTK

**2. The Five-Burst**
- Fire 5 rounds, pull down firmly
- Best at 20-35m
- Sweet spot for the AKM

**3. The Spray (Meta Build Only)**
- Full auto with zero-recoil build
- Only effective within 25m
- Requires all recoil attachments

<Callout type="warning" title="Critical Mistake">
Never full-auto spray the AKM beyond 30m without the meta build. You'll waste your entire magazine and die.
</Callout>

## AKM Pro Tips from Top Players

### Tip #1: Respect the Recoil
The AKM is not an M7. Don't expect to spray and pray. **Every shot must be intentional.**

### Tip #2: Pair with an SMG
The AKM covers 25-50m. Pair it with an SMG for 0-20m:
- **AKM:** Mid-range dominance
- **SMG:** Close-quarters insurance

### Tip #3: Pre-Aim at Head Level
With 42 damage, **two headshots = kill** at most ranges. Pre-aim head level at common angles.

### Tip #4: Use Cover Between Bursts
The AKM rewards **peek-and-shoot** playstyle. Use cover to reset between bursts.

### Tip #5: Learn to Tap-Fire
At 50m+, switch to single tap-fire. The AKM's damage means **one well-placed shot** does massive work.
