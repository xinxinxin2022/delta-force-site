## S10 Season Meta Overview

Season 10 has brought two major shake-ups to the weapon meta:

1. **SVCH marksman rifle** — A new 7.62×54R DMR that arrived with S10 and immediately claimed top-tier status. Its combination of high per-shot damage, manageable recoil (especially with the Gen 3 grip), and excellent range makes it the most powerful new weapon since the M7's debut.

2. **RM277 assault rifle** — Another S10 newcomer, the RM277 offers competitive performance with the M7 but with a slightly different recoil pattern. It's already seeing adoption in All-Out Warfare ranked play.

Meanwhile, the **AS VAL** has surged to T0 following recent buffs, and the **M14** is making a strong comeback thanks to its resonance grip attachment that solves its historical recoil problems.

## Why These Weapons? The Meta Logic

### The M7 problem

The M7 is the only weapon that is T0 in both modes, and it has been since early access. The reason is simple: it has no bad range, no bad situation, and no major weakness. It does not have the highest DPS, the fastest TTK, or the largest magazine — but it is *good enough at everything* that it is almost never the wrong pick. Until the devs nerf its consistency, it will stay at the top.

### AS VAL: The Aggressive King

The AS VAL's rise to T0 is the biggest meta shift of S9/S10. Its integral suppressor means you never give away your position, its close-range TTK rivals any SMG, and its damage dropoff at range is less severe than most players expect. In the hands of an aggressive player, the AS VAL is arguably more lethal than the M7.

### MK47 and M14: The Precision Duo

These two sit at T0.5 in Havoc because they both reward skill more than the M7 does. The **MK47** is even more demanding: its burst fire punishes panic-spraying, but in the hands of a precise player it deletes enemies faster than anything else in its class. The **M14** is making a strong comeback — the Gen 3 resonance grip attachment finally solved its historical recoil issues, turning it from a niche pick into a legitimate meta contender.

### SVCH: The New DMR Meta

The SVCH marksman rifle is Season 10's headline weapon. Chambered in 7.62×54R, it delivers devastating damage at mid-to-long range with manageable recoil. In Havoc, it's the ideal support weapon for squad pushes. In All-Out Warfare, a single SVCH player can lock down entire sightlines and deny enemy rotations. Still being theory-crafted, but early signs point to T0 potential.

### SMGs: MP7 and P90

SMGs rise in All-Out Warfare because the mode's pacing favors close-quarters fights. The **MP7** is the Havoc SMG of choice because it also performs respectably at mid-range when you get caught out. The **P90** is pure close-quarters dominance, with a magazine that lets you fight through multiple enemies without reloading.

## FAQ

### What is the best weapon in Delta Force Hawk Ops right now?
The **M7** remains the best all-round weapon in both Havoc and All-Out Warfare. However, the **AS VAL** has surged to equal T0 status after recent buffs, especially for aggressive close-to-mid range play.

### Is the SVCH good in Season 10?
Yes. The SVCH marksman rifle is the standout new weapon of S10. It's already T1 with strong T0 potential as the community optimizes builds. Excellent for mid-long range engagements in both modes.

### What is the best budget weapon for beginners?
The **QJB201** at ~60K is the best budget weapon for beginners. It's cheap, reliable, has a huge magazine, and performs well in both Havoc and All-Out Warfare. The **CAR-15** (~150K) is the next step up.

### Is the AKM still good in 2026?
The AKM has fallen from T0.5 to T2 in the current meta. It's outclassed by the M7 and AS VAL in most situations. However, it's still a solid budget pick at ~200K and remains forgiving for newer players.

### What weapons should I avoid?
Avoid the **SG552** in Havoc Operations — it underperforms at typical engagement ranges and the ammo cost doesn't justify the DPS. The **AKS74** is also outclassed in every relevant stat by better alternatives.

### How often does the weapon meta change?
The meta shifts with every balance patch, typically every 4-6 weeks. Major changes come with new seasons (every ~2 months). We update this tier list after every significant patch.
