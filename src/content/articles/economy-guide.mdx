Delta Force: Hawk Ops has one of the most player-driven-friendly economies in a modern extraction shooter. The currency is **Havoc Coins**, the exchange rate is **10 diamonds = 1M coins**, and every run you complete is a chance to print money — or lose your shirt. This guide breaks down the full loop: how to earn, where to spend, and what to upgrade first.

Whether you're a fresh Level 7 player trying to fund your first proper loadout or a veteran optimizing your coin-per-hour, the strategies below will keep your wallet healthy in 2026.

## Currency System: Havoc Coins & Diamonds

Everything in Havoc Operations runs on **Havoc Coins**. The premium track is diamonds, but the game is generous with the conversion: **10 diamonds = 1,000,000 Havoc Coins**. Most players never need to touch diamonds — smart knife running and market play will fund everything you need.

<Callout type="warning" title="The 5% Market Handling Fee">
Every sale on the player market is taxed at **5%**. This adds up fast if you're flipping low-value loot. Focus on high-ticket items (room-key loot, weapon mods, rare attachments) where the fee doesn't eat your margin.
</Callout>

## Knife Run Routes & Earnings

"Knife running" — going in with minimal gear and extracting maximum loot — is the single most coin-efficient strategy in the game. Here are the three core routes ranked by ROI:

### Dam Zero Express (8–12 min / 300K–500K)

The starter money-maker. Short, low-risk, and reliable. You won't get rich on a single run, but you can chain 4–5 in an hour for **1.2M–2.5M coins** with near-zero gear risk. Perfect for warming up or rebuilding after a bad run.

### Cement Factory Route (~20 min / 800K–1.5M)

The mid-game workhorse. Longer than Dam Zero but the loot quality is meaningfully better. A successful run here pays for a full mid-tier loadout. Recommended once you have the map memorized and a consistent squad.

### Longbow Quality Run (~35 min / 500K–2M)

The complex-map knife run. Uses the Longbow Valley 35-minute timer to hit the Diamond Queen Hotel and Radar Station. Higher variance than the other routes, but the ceiling is 2M+ coins on a good pull. Read our [Longbow Valley guide](/articles/longbow-valley-map.html) for the exact path.

## Classified Mode: The "Skunk Set" Strategy

In **Classified mode** (the highest tier of Havoc Operations), veteran players run the infamous **"Skunk Set"** — a card-load strategy optimized for maximum loot-per-hour at minimum detection risk. The Skunk Set combines:

- **Noise-dampening cards** that hide your movement from other squads.
- **Inventory expansion cards** so you can carry more loot per trip.
- **Extract-speed cards** to reduce the time you're vulnerable at extraction points.

The Skunk Set isn't about winning fights — it's about **never being in one**. You in, you loot, you're gone before other teams realize you were there. This strategy prints money over long sessions but requires patience and map knowledge.

## Spending Priority: Where Your Coins Should Go

New players waste coins on the wrong things. Here's the spending hierarchy, in order:

1. **Weapon Mods (Top Priority)** — The single biggest performance boost. A well-modded AKM beats a stock meta weapon every time.
2. **Ammo** — Never run into a complex map with low-tier ammo. The ROI on high-tier ammo is massive because it lets you win the fights that protect your loot.
3. **Armor** — Buy what you can afford to lose. Running a 500K armor set on a 50K knife run is the #1 beginner mistake.
4. **Medical Supplies** — Cheap insurance. Don't skimp here; a 10K medkit can save a 500K run.

<Callout type="success" title="The 70/20/10 Rule">
Spend 70% of your income on weapon mods, 20% on ammo and armor, and 10% on facility upgrades. Flip the order once your facilities hit tier 3 — at that point, upgrades become the best ROI in the game.
</Callout>

## Trading Tips & Market Play

The player market is where patient players make their biggest margins. A few tips:

- **Buy at reset, sell at peak.** Prices dip right after daily/weekly resets. Stock up on ammo and mods then, sell when demand peaks mid-cycle.
- **Track room key prices.** A key that's cheap today might be worth 10x next week if the meta shifts. Don't be afraid to hold inventory.
- **Watch the patch notes.** Balance changes swing the market hard. When a weapon gets nerfed, its attachments tank in price — buy the dip.

## Final Thoughts

The Delta Force economy rewards consistency over heroism. Chain knife runs, upgrade your facilities in order, spend on mods first, and never bring more than you can afford to lose. Follow this loop and you'll be funding full meta loadouts within your first week.
