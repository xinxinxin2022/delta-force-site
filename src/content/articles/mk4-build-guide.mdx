## MK4 Recoil Pattern & Control Tips

The MK4 has a **predictable vertical climb** with slight horizontal drift. Here's how to master it:

### Recoil Pattern Breakdown

```
Shots 1-5:   Strong vertical climb (pull down 30%)
Shots 6-15:  Vertical + slight right drift (pull down-left)
Shots 16-30: Stabilizes with attachment build
```

### Control Techniques

1. **Burst Fire (5-7 rounds):** Most accurate for mid-range
2. **Tap Fire (2-3 rounds):** Best for long-range headshots
3. **Full Auto:** Only with zero-recoil build, close range

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistake">
Don't full-auto spray beyond 40m without the meta build. You'll miss 80% of shots.
</Callout>

## MK4 Pro Tips from Top Players

After reviewing **70K+ views** of build guides and community feedback, here are the top tips:

### Tip #1: Master the Burst
The MK4's sweet spot is **5-7 round bursts** at mid-range. Full-auto sprays waste ammo and reveal your position.

### Tip #2: Pair with an SMG
Run MK4 as primary, SMG as secondary. This covers all engagement distances:
- **MK4:** 30-60m engagements
- **SMG:** 0-20m close quarters

### Tip #3: Pre-Aim Common Angles
The MK4's ADS speed is good but not instant. Pre-aim doorways and common flanking routes to gain the first-shot advantage.

### Tip #4: Manage Your Ammo
With 30-40 rounds, you can sustain 2-3 firefights before reloading. **Reload during rotations**, not mid-fight.

### Tip #5: Use Cover Effectively
The MK4 rewards **peek-and-shoot** playstyle. Use cover to reset your recoil pattern between engagements.
