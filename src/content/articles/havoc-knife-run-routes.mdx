## Knife Run Fundamentals

Before diving into specific routes, let's establish the core principles of successful knife running.

### Essential Gear

For optimal knife runs, bring only what you absolutely need:

- **Primary weapon**: Cheapest reliable SMG or pistol (Uzi or stock pistol)
- **Armor**: Light armor at most, often none for maximum speed
- **Backpack**: Medium or large backpack for loot capacity
- **Medical**: 1-2 bandages only
- **Optional**: Smoke grenades for emergency escapes

Total gear cost should be under 5,000 credits per run.

### Timing Principles

Successful knife runs depend on understanding match timing:

- **0-2 minutes**: Landing phase, other teams spreading out
- **2-5 minutes**: Initial looting phase, teams busy with AI and loot
- **5-10 minutes**: Mid-game, teams starting to engage each other
- **10+ minutes**: Late game, remaining teams hunting or extracting

The optimal knife run window is 2-5 minutes after landing. This gives you time to reach your target while other teams are distracted.

<Callout type="tip" title="Speed is Everything">
Every second matters in knife running. Practice your routes until they become muscle memory. The difference between profit and death often comes down to 5-10 seconds of faster movement.
</Callout>

## Dam Zero Knife Run Routes

Dam Zero is the most popular map for knife running due to its compact size and high-value loot locations. Here are three optimized routes:

### Route 1: Central Market Blitz

**Risk Level**: Medium
**Expected Loot Value**: 45,000-65,000 credits
**Time to Complete**: 3-4 minutes
**Extraction**: North Bridge or Underground Tunnel

**Step-by-step path**:

1. **Spawn and sprint north** (0:00-0:30) — Head directly toward the Central Market area
2. **Enter market through east door** (0:30-1:00) — Use the eastern entrance to avoid common spawn camping spots
3. **Loot ground floor stalls** (1:00-1:45) — Focus on electronics crates and weapon racks
4. **Check upper level** (1:45-2:30) — Quick sweep of second floor for high-value items
5. **Exit through west side** (2:30-3:00) — Avoid the main entrance where you came in
6. **Rotate to extraction** (3:00-4:00) — Head to North Bridge or Underground Tunnel depending on spawn

**Key tips**:

- The Central Market has multiple high-value spawn points on both floors
- Avoid spending more than 45 seconds on each floor
- If you hear gunfire, abort and extract immediately
- The underground tunnel extraction is safer but slower than the north bridge

### Route 2: Military Compound Dash

**Risk Level**: High
**Expected Loot Value**: 70,000-95,000 credits
**Time to Complete**: 4-5 minutes
**Extraction**: East Road or South Road

**Step-by-step path**:

1. **Spawn and head east** (0:00-0:45) — Sprint toward the Military Compound
2. **Enter through south gate** (0:45-1:15) — Use the southern entrance for less exposure
3. **Loot armory** (1:15-2:00) — High-value weapon spawns and military gear
4. **Check command center** (2:00-2:45) — Electronics and intel items
5. **Grab vehicle bay loot** (2:45-3:30) — Additional high-value spawns
6. **Extract via east or south road** (3:30-5:00) — Choose based on enemy positions

**Risk assessment**: The Military Compound attracts other teams due to high-value loot. Expect potential encounters, but the loot value justifies the risk for experienced players.

## Extraction Strategies After Knife Runs

Successful extraction is just as important as successful looting. Here are strategies for each extraction type:

### Safe Extraction Principles

- **Don't linger**: Once you have your loot, extract immediately
- **Use alternative routes**: Avoid the same paths other teams might expect
- **Check extraction points**: If possible, use Luna's drone or scout before committing
- **Have backup plans**: Know multiple extraction routes for each map
- **Time your extraction**: Late-game extractions (8+ minutes) are safer as fewer teams remain

### Avoiding Extraction Campers

Extraction campers are a constant threat. Counter them with these strategies:

- **Use less popular extractions**: The Underground Tunnel and Emergency Exit are less camped
- **Approach from unexpected angles**: Use alternative routes to reach extraction points
- **Scout before committing**: Check for campers before moving to extract
- **Have escape plans**: If you spot campers, know your retreat routes
- **Coordinate with teammates**: If playing in a squad, have one player scout while others wait

## Profit Optimization Tips

To maximize your knife running efficiency, follow these advanced tips:

### Route Cycling

Don't run the same route every match. Cycle through different routes to:

- Avoid predictability that other players might exploit
- Experience different loot spawns
- Learn multiple extraction routes
- Adapt to different match conditions

### Squad Knife Running

Running with a squad can increase efficiency:

- **Split up**: Each player takes a different section of the POI
- **Cover each other**: One player watches while others loot
- **Share loot**: Distribute high-value items among squad members
- **Coordinate extraction**: Extract together for safety

### Risk Management

Understand when to push and when to abort:

- **If you hear gunfire nearby**: Abort and extract with what you have
- **If you see multiple teams**: Consider aborting or finding alternative routes
- **If you're low on health**: Extract immediately, don't risk death
- **If extraction is contested**: Use alternative extraction points
