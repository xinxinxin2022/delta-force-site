## Top 20 High-Value Loot Locations

### Tier 1: Elite Loot Spawns (Rank 1-5)

These locations consistently spawn the highest-value items but are also the most contested.

**1. Military Bunkers — Dam Zero**

- **Expected Value**: 80,000-150,000 credits
- **Risk Level**: Very High
- **Best Approach**: Enter from underground tunnels to avoid common chokepoints
- **Key Loot**: Intelligence documents, military equipment, artifacts
- **Extraction Strategy**: Use the east extraction point for fastest exit

The underground market in Bakhsh is a treasure trove of gold items and artifacts. The tunnel system added in Season 8 provides safer approach routes, but the actual market area is a death trap with limited cover and multiple entry points. Clear the area thoroughly before looting, and always have an escape route planned.

## Risk vs Reward Analysis

Understanding the risk-reward balance is crucial for maximizing your profits in Havoc Operations. Here's a breakdown of how to approach different tiers of loot locations:

### High Risk, High Reward (Tier 1-2)

**When to attempt**:

- You have a coordinated squad with good communication
- You're running full loadouts with superior firepower
- You've scouted the area and know the current contest level
- You have a clear extraction plan

**When to avoid**:

- You're running solo or with an uncoordinated squad
- You're on a knife run with minimal gear
- The location is heavily contested (3+ squads)
- You don't have a clear extraction route

<Callout type="warning" title="Know Your Limits">
High-value loot locations are tempting, but they're also death traps if you're not prepared. Only attempt these locations if you're confident in your squad's ability to handle multiple engagements and extract safely.
</Callout>

### Medium Risk, Medium Reward (Tier 3)

**When to attempt**:

- You have a small squad (2-3 players)
- You're running balanced loadouts
- You want good loot without extreme contest rates
- You have multiple extraction options

**When to avoid**:

- You're running solo and the location is contested
- You're on a tight timeline and can't afford delays
- You don't have good cover for approach and extraction

### Low Risk, Low Reward (Tier 4)

**When to attempt**:

- You're running solo or with a small squad
- You're on a knife run with minimal gear
- You want consistent, safe loot without high contest
- You're filling your backpack with basic supplies

**When to avoid**:

- You need high-value loot to meet your profit goals
- You have time for higher-risk runs
- You're running full loadouts and want maximum value

## Map-Specific Loot Strategies

Each map has unique characteristics that affect loot strategies. Here are map-specific tips for maximizing your profits:

### Dam Zero

**Characteristics**: Compact map, multiple high-value locations, high contest rates

**Strategy**:

- Prioritize speed over thoroughness — loot quickly and extract
- Use the underground tunnel system for safe rotation
- Target the Military Bunkers early if you have a coordinated squad
- Have multiple extraction routes planned based on your position

**Best locations**: Military Bunkers, Central Control Tower, Warehouse District

### Longbow Valley

**Characteristics**: Large open map, terrain cover, moderate contest rates

**Strategy**:

- Use terrain cover for all movement — avoid open areas
- Target high-value locations early before other squads arrive
- Have clear extraction routes planned based on your position
- Be prepared for long-range engagements during approach and extraction

**Best locations**: Military Outpost, Radio Tower Compound, Supply Depot

### Bakhsh

**Characteristics**: Vertical map, complex layout, moderate-high contest rates

**Strategy**:

- Use the scaffolding and tunnel system for safe vertical movement
- Target upper-level locations for highest value
- Be prepared for close-quarters combat in market areas
- Use the side alley extraction for safest exit

**Best locations**: Central Market Upper Levels, Bakhsh Market Underground, Residential Compound

### Aerospace Base

**Characteristics**: Large interior spaces, mixed contest rates, multiple extraction options

**Strategy**:

- Use the new elevator and expanded areas for safe movement
- Target high-value locations like the Assembly Line early
- Be prepared for medium-range engagements in hangar areas
- Use the emergency extraction for fastest exit

**Best locations**: Aerospace Assembly Line, Control Tower Upper Floors, Fuel Depot Storage

## Squad Loot Coordination

Running with a squad allows you to coordinate loot runs for maximum efficiency. Here are strategies for squad loot coordination:

### The Split Strategy

**How it works**: Split your squad into two teams, each targeting different locations

**Benefits**:

- Cover more ground in less time
- Reduce contest at each location
- Provide mutual support if one team gets engaged

**Execution**:

- Team A targets Tier 1-2 locations (high value, high risk)
- Team B targets Tier 3-4 locations (medium value, low risk)
- Coordinate extraction timing and locations
- Share loot if one team has spare backpack space

### The Stack Strategy

**How it works**: Keep your squad together and hit locations as a unit

**Benefits**:

- Superior firepower for contested locations
- Better coordination and communication
- Easier to defend during extraction

**Execution**:

- Target Tier 1-2 locations with full squad firepower
- Clear locations thoroughly before looting
- One player watches while others loot
- Extract together for maximum safety

### The Decoy Strategy

**How it works**: One player acts as a decoy while others loot

**Benefits**:

- Draw enemy attention away from looting players
- Create opportunities for safe extraction
- Maximize loot collection time

**Execution**:

- One player creates noise and draws enemy attention
- Other players loot high-value locations
- Decoy player extracts separately or rejoins for extraction
- Requires good communication and timing
