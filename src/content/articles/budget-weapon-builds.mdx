## How to Import Build Share Codes

Each build below includes a share code. To import:

1. Open the **Weapon Modification** screen from the loadout menu.
2. Select the weapon the build is for.
3. Choose **Import Build** and paste the share code.
4. The game will apply all attachments and mods automatically. Verify the total cost matches your budget.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
Share codes can be patched by balance changes. If a code no longer applies cleanly, check this guide again — we update after every major patch.
</Callout>

## AKM — The ~200K Budget King

The AKM is the cheapest meta-competitive rifle in the game. At around **200,000** fully built, it trades a little recoil control for a lot of per-bullet damage — making it one of the most lethal rifles at mid-range when you can control it.

### Share code

`6GQBM280BAC7RIM3B0293`

### Build philosophy

This build prioritizes **damage and vertical recoil control** over handling. You get the AKM hitting as hard as the mods allow, with enough recoil mitigation that controlled bursts are consistent. The tradeoff is slightly slower ADS and heavier movement — do not try to run-and-gun with it.

| Slot | Pick | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Muzzle | Compensator | Cuts vertical recoil — the AKM's biggest weakness |
| Barrel | Long Barrel | Extends damage range and bullet velocity |
| Optic | 2x–4x ACOG | The AKM shines at mid-range; give it the glass to match |
| Grip | Vertical Foregrip | More vertical recoil control, minimal handling penalty |
| Magazine | Extended 40 | 30 rounds run out fast with the AKM's fire rate |
| Ammunition | FMJ | Better penetration and damage vs. armored targets in Havoc |

### Pros

- Cheapest meta build in the game.
- Per-bullet damage rivals T0 weapons.
- Simple to play — point and burst.

### Cons

- Recoil is rough at full auto. Commit to bursts.
- Slow ADS — do not take close-quarters fights without a sidearm plan.
- Outclassed by MK47 and M7 at longer ranges.

<Callout type="success" title="Best For">
Players building their first Havoc loadout, anyone losing gear often, and budget-focused squads running a high-damage comp.
</Callout>

## MK47 — The ~300K Precision Pick

The MK47 sits in the uncomfortable gap between assault rifle and marksman rifle. This build leans fully into **precision** — turning the MK47 into a semi-auto that deletes enemies in two well-placed shots at mid-range, with enough versatility to handle close fights when you are careful.

### Share code

`6IO0P480B3NT80JEK12E8`

### Build philosophy

Maximize **accuracy, range, and per-shot damage**. This is not a spray build — it is a build for players who trust their aim and want each shot to count. Recoil is managed for tap-fire and short bursts, not sustained auto.

| Slot | Pick | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Muzzle | Precision Suppressor | Tightens shot grouping and keeps you off the minimap |
| Barrel | Marksman Barrel | Extends effective range significantly |
| Optic | 3x–6x Variable | Lets you engage confidently out to 80m |
| Grip | Bipod Grip | Stabilizes shots when prone or braced — massive accuracy boost |
| Magazine | Default 20 | Keeps weight and ADS down; you should not need more |
| Ammunition | Match Grade | Tightest possible grouping at range |

### Pros

- Two-shot kills at mid-range against unarmored targets.
- Excellent at holding angles and long sightlines.
- Suppressor keeps you stealthy for rotations in Havoc.

### Cons

- Demands good aim — punish harsh if you panic-spray.
- Small magazine means you must commit to every shot.
- Slow handling in close-quarters; pair with an SMG sidearm.

<Callout type="warning" title="Skill Warning">
The MK47 has the highest skill floor of the three builds in this guide. If you miss shots, you lose. Practice on the range before bringing it into ranked Havoc.
</Callout>

## QJB201 — The 60K–340K Budget LMG

The QJB201 is the most flexible weapon in the game because it can be built for anywhere from **60,000** to **340,000** depending on how much you want to spend. The budget version is a surprisingly effective suppression tool; the fully-kitted version competes with much more expensive rifles.

### Share code (mid-budget build)

`6J2R7JC0BAC7RIM3B02`

### Build philosophy

The mid-budget build targets **magazine capacity and sustained-fire stability**. You get a huge magazine, manageable recoil for an LMG, and enough range to be useful across the map. Strip mods to drop to 60K; go max to reach 340K.

| Slot | Pick | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Muzzle | Heavy Compensator | LMG recoil needs all the help it can get |
| Barrel | Heavy Long Barrel | Better range and damage falloff — essential for LMG play |
| Optic | 2x–4x ACOG | Default iron sights are too cluttered for sustained fire |
| Grip | Angle Grip | Balances horizontal recoil without killing ADS |
| Magazine | Extended 75 | The QJB201's biggest selling point — huge mag, stay in fights |
| Ammunition | FMJ | Penetration for Havoc armored targets |

### Pros

- Flexible price point — works at any economy level.
- Huge magazine lets you win multi-enemy fights.
- Surprisingly viable at range when built correctly.

### Cons

- Heavy — you will be slower than rifle players.
- ADS is slow; do not get caught peeking close.
- At max build cost, the M7 does the same job better.

<Callout type="success" title="Best For">
Players who want one weapon that scales with their economy, support players laying fire for their squad, and Havoc squads that need a budget suppression option.
</Callout>

## Final Recommendations

- Just starting out? Build the **AKM** first. It is cheap, powerful, and teaches recoil control that transfers to every other rifle.
- Confident in your aim? Save for the **MK47**. It rewards precision more than any other budget weapon.
- Playing Havoc support or on a tight economy? The **QJB201** at 60K lets you compete without breaking the bank.
