## Bakhsh: Museum & Babel Tower

Bakhsh is a two-part complex map: the sprawling **Museum** on the west side and the vertical **Babel Tower** on the east. Most teams specialize in one half — trying to clear both in a single run is possible but risky. The map rewards patience and positional awareness over raw speed.

### Babel Tower Control Zone (T0)

The iconic centerpiece of Bakhsh. The tower has three named sub-zones that every player needs to learn by name — they're the callouts you'll hear in squad comms:

- **Ocean Hall** — the ground-floor lobby with medium-tier loot and heavy AI presence.
- **Interrogation Zone** — mid-tower locked rooms with high-value intel spawns.
- **"Three Red Phantoms"** — the top-floor trio of rooms. Highest value, highest risk. Expect company.

<Callout type="warning" title="Tower Extraction — Pull-Switch">
The Babel Tower uses a **pull-switch extraction** that starts a **4-minute countdown** once activated. You cannot leave immediately — you have to defend the switch or sprint to an alternate exit. Plan your escape before you pull it.
</Callout>

### Museum Complex

The Museum is a horizontal maze of exhibition halls, storage rooms, and secret passages. It has **8 major free safe points** — loot spots that don't require keys and reliably drop mid-to-high tier items. The Museum is also less popular than the tower, making it a smart target for teams that want consistent value without the PvP gauntlet.

## Aerospace Base: Highest Resource Density

Aerospace Base is the **richest map in the game** by pure loot volume. It has three core zones, a Prison Zone, a Pump Room with ~22 individual loot points, and the infamous **CEO Office** — a speedrun target for veteran teams.

### The 3 Core Zones

1. **Control Building Top Floor (T0)** — The classic "high ground, high reward" zone. Weapon mods, armor, and rare attachments spawn here consistently.
2. **Launch Area Fuel Depot (T0–T1)** — A large open zone with cover and heavy loot. Dangerous because of sightlines — bring a long-range loadout.
3. **Centrifuge Room Underground (T0)** — The highest-value single room in Aerospace. Getting there requires navigating lower levels, which means you're committed once you go in.

### Prison Zone & Pump Room

The Prison Zone is an often-overlooked area with **~22 individual loot points**. It's less contested than the core zones and great for teams running a "loot everything" strategy. The adjacent **Pump Room** is a compact but rich area — clear it quickly and rotate.

### CEO Office Speedrun

The CEO Office is the speedrun target of Aerospace Base. A coordinated squad can spawn, clear a path, loot the office, and extract in under 10 minutes. The loot quality justifies the investment — but **only attempt this once you know the map cold**.

<Callout type="success" title="Pro Strategy">
Split a 3-person squad: two players push the Centrifuge Room while one holds the Control Building stairwell as overwatch. If the Centrifuge team gets pinned, the overwatch player can cover their extraction.
</Callout>

## Extraction Route Summary

- **Bakhsh:** The tower pull-switch is the fastest extraction from Babel Tower but commits you to a 4-minute defense. Plan a fallback exit if you're solo.
- **Aerospace Base:** Use the north extract for Control Building runs, east extract for Fuel Depot, and south extract for Centrifuge Room.
- On both maps, **always leave 8+ minutes** on the timer before heading to extraction.

## Which Map Should You Run?

If you're still building your economy, **Bakhsh's Museum** is the safer pick — consistent loot, less player density. If you're geared up and confident in your gunskill, **Aerospace Base** offers the highest ceiling in the game. The best teams rotate between both maps depending on their loadout budget and squad size.
