### Understanding the Numbers

**Damage**: The MP5 has the highest damage per shot (28), making it effective at slightly longer ranges. The Vector has the lowest damage (22) but compensates with extreme fire rate.

**Fire Rate**: The Vector dominates with 1200 RPM, creating an incredibly fast time-to-kill at close range. The Uzi has the slowest fire rate (750 RPM) but is still faster than most ARs.

**Effective Range**: The MP5 has the longest effective range (35m), making it the most versatile SMG. The Vector falls off sharply after 25m, limiting it to pure CQC.

**ADS Speed**: The Vector and Uzi have the fastest ADS speeds, making them the most responsive in close quarters. The MP5 and P90 are slightly slower but still very fast.

**Magazine Size**: The P90 dominates with 50+1 rounds, providing exceptional sustained fire capability. The Vector has the smallest magazine (25+1), requiring frequent reloads given its high fire rate.

## Individual SMG Breakdowns

### Vector — The Close-Range Destroyer

The Vector is the ultimate close-quarters weapon. With the highest fire rate in the game (1200 RPM), it melts targets at point-blank range. However, its limited range and small magazine make it highly situational.

<Screenshot src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/guoyx/delta-force-site@images-v1/images/vector-smg-loadout.jpg" alt="Vector SMG close-quarters loadout and attachments" />

**Strengths**:

- Highest fire rate in the game (1200 RPM)
- Fastest ADS speed for instant target acquisition
- Devastating time-to-kill at close range (0.40 seconds)
- Extremely responsive in tight spaces

**Weaknesses**:

- Smallest magazine (25+1) requires frequent reloads
- Severe damage falloff after 25 meters
- Limited versatility — only effective in CQC
- Season 8 damage nerf reduced its one-shot potential
- High ammunition consumption

**Best Loadout**:

- **Optic**: Red dot sight (holographic for faster target acquisition)
- **Barrel**: Suppressor (for stealth) or compensator (for recoil control)
- **Underbarrel**: Angled grip (faster ADS) or vertical grip (recoil control)
- **Magazine**: Extended magazine (essential — brings it to 35+1)
- **Stock**: Lightweight stock (improved mobility)

**Modification Code**: `Vector-CQC-Melt` (example code for optimal setup)

<Callout type="warning" title="Vector Magazine Management">
The Vector's small magazine and extreme fire rate mean you'll burn through ammo incredibly fast. Always carry extra magazines and reload during lulls in combat. The extended magazine attachment is not optional — it's essential for the Vector to be viable.
</Callout>

**Best For**: Aggressive players who excel at close-quarters combat and can manage the Vector's limitations. Ideal for tight maps like Dam Zero where engagements are almost always under 25 meters.

### MP5 — The Versatile Workhorse

The MP5 is the most balanced and versatile SMG. With good damage, manageable recoil, and the longest effective range in the category, it can handle a wider variety of situations than other SMGs.

<Screenshot src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/guoyx/delta-force-site@images-v1/images/mp5-smg-loadout.jpg" alt="MP5 SMG versatile loadout and attachments" />

**Strengths**:

- Highest damage per shot (28) in the SMG category
- Longest effective range (35m) for an SMG
- Low recoil makes it easy to control
- Season 8 buffs improved its range and damage
- Versatile performance across most close-to-medium situations

**Weaknesses**:

- Fire rate is lower than Vector and P90
- Not as fast as Vector or Uzi in pure CQC
- Less specialized — good at everything but doesn't excel at anything

**Best Loadout**:

- **Optic**: Red dot or 2x scope (versatility for slightly longer engagements)
- **Barrel**: Suppressor (stealth) or long barrel (increased range)
- **Underbarrel**: Vertical grip (recoil control) or angled grip (ADS speed)
- **Magazine**: Extended magazine (brings it to 40+1 for sustained fire)
- **Stock**: Tactical stock (improved handling)

**Modification Code**: `MP5-Versatile-Workhorse` (example code)

**Best For**: Players who want an SMG that can handle most close-quarters situations while maintaining some effectiveness at slightly longer ranges. Ideal for players transitioning from ARs to SMGs.


<Callout type="info" title="MP5 Meta Position">
The MP5's Season 8 buffs have made it the most versatile SMG in the game. The increased damage and range make it viable in situations where other SMGs would struggle. It's the safest choice for players who want reliable SMG performance.
</Callout>

### Uzi — The Aggressive Speedster

The Uzi is all about speed and aggression. With very fast ADS speed and improved range control from Season 8, it's an excellent choice for players who like to push aggressively and reposition quickly.

**Strengths**:

- Very fast ADS speed for responsive handling
- Season 8 changes improved range control and forgiveness
- Low recoil makes it easy to control during aggressive pushes
- Good mobility for fast repositioning
- Manageable fire rate conserves ammo better than Vector

**Weaknesses**:

- Season 8 fire rate reduction (-50 RPM) reduced its CQC dominance
- Damage is lower than MP5
- Less effective at range than MP5
- Not as devastating as Vector in pure CQC

**Best Loadout**:

- **Optic**: Red dot sight (fast target acquisition)
- **Barrel**: Suppressor (stealth for aggressive pushes)
- **Underbarrel**: Angled grip (even faster ADS) or vertical grip (recoil control)
- **Magazine**: Extended magazine (brings it to 40+1)
- **Stock**: Lightweight stock (maximum mobility)

**Modification Code**: `Uzi-Aggressive-Speed` (example code)

**Best For**: Aggressive players who prioritize speed and mobility over raw damage. The Uzi's improved range control in Season 8 makes it more forgiving for players who push aggressively.

### P90 — The Sustained Fire Specialist

The P90 is unique among SMGs with its massive 50+1 round magazine. This gives it exceptional sustained fire capability, allowing you to engage multiple targets without reloading. However, Season 8's range reduction has limited its versatility.

<Screenshot src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/guoyx/delta-force-site@images-v1/images/p90-smg-loadout.jpg" alt="P90 SMG sustained fire loadout and attachments" />

**Strengths**:

- Largest magazine (50+1) for exceptional sustained fire
- Good fire rate (850 RPM) balances damage and ammo consumption
- Low-medium recoil makes it controllable
- Can engage multiple targets without reloading
- Good for aggressive pushes where you can't afford to reload

**Weaknesses**:

- Season 8 range reduction (-3m) limits its versatility
- Damage is lower than MP5
- Slower ADS speed than Vector and Uzi
- Now strictly defined as a close-range option

**Best Loadout**:

- **Optic**: Red dot or holographic sight
- **Barrel**: Compensator (recoil control for sustained fire)
- **Underbarrel**: Vertical grip (recoil control) or angled grip (ADS speed)
- **Magazine**: Standard magazine (already 50+1, no need to extend)
- **Stock**: Tactical stock (improved handling)

**Modification Code**: `P90-Sustained-Fire` (example code)

**Best For**: Players who want to sustain fire through multiple engagements without reloading. The P90's large magazine makes it ideal for aggressive players who push into enemy positions and need to handle multiple targets.

## CQC Tactics and Positioning

SMGs excel in close-quarters, but success requires understanding positioning and tactics.

### Room Clearing Fundamentals

**Pre-fire common angles**: When entering a room, pre-fire corners and common camping spots. Your fast ADS speed gives you an advantage in reactive gunfights.

**Slice the pie**: Clear rooms by slicing the pie — methodically clearing angles as you move through doorways. Your fast handling makes this technique very effective.

**Use momentum**: SMGs reward aggressive movement. Don't hesitate — push into rooms with confidence, using your speed advantage to overwhelm enemies.

**Reload management**: Always reload behind cover. SMG magazines empty quickly, and getting caught reloading in the open is fatal.

### Positioning Principles

**Stay close**: SMGs are most effective under 25 meters. Position yourself to force close-range engagements.

**Use cover aggressively**: Push from cover to cover, always having a hard surface between you and potential threats. Your fast ADS speed means you can peek and shoot effectively.

**Flank and reposition**: SMGs' mobility makes them ideal for flanking maneuvers. Use your speed to get unexpected angles on enemies.

**Avoid open areas**: Never cross open ground when possible. SMGs are vulnerable at range, and open areas expose you to longer-range weapons.

<Callout type="tip" title="SMG Positioning Golden Rule">
If you can see an enemy at 40+ meters and they can see you, you're at a disadvantage. Reposition, use cover, or switch to a longer-range weapon. SMGs win fights under 25 meters — force those engagements.
</Callout>

### When to Switch Weapons

**Enemy at long range**: If an enemy is consistently engaging you from 40+ meters, switch to an AR, marksman rifle, or sniper.

**Open map areas**: On maps like Longbow Valley where engagements often happen at 50+ meters, consider running an AR as your primary and SMG as secondary.

**Defensive positions**: When holding angles at medium range, an AR or marksman rifle is more effective than an SMG.

**Sustained long-range fights**: If you find yourself in a prolonged firefight at 30+ meters, your SMG is the wrong tool.

## Best SMG Per Map and Mode

### Havoc Operations

**Dam Zero** (Tight corridors, close-quarters):

- **Best**: Vector or MP5
- **Why**: Almost all engagements are under 25m, perfect for SMGs
- **Strategy**: Run SMG as primary, push aggressively through buildings

**Longbow Valley** (Open terrain, longer sight lines):

- **Best**: MP5 (as secondary)
- **Why**: Longbow favors longer-range weapons; use SMG for building clearing
- **Strategy**: Run AR or marksman as primary, SMG for close-quarters backup

**Bakhsh** (Mixed, vertical combat):

- **Best**: MP5 or Uzi
- **Why**: Mixed engagement ranges; MP5's versatility handles both
- **Strategy**: Use SMG for building combat, switch to longer-range for open areas

**Aerospace Base** (Large interiors, mixed ranges):

- **Best**: MP5 or P90
- **Why**: Large interiors with occasional longer sight lines
- **Strategy**: SMG for interior combat, have longer-range option for hangar areas

### All-Out Warfare

**Objective Push**:

- **Best**: Vector or Uzi
- **Why**: Fast handling for aggressive objective pushes
- **Strategy**: Use speed to close distance and overwhelm defenders

**Objective Defense**:

- **Best**: MP5
- **Why**: Versatility handles both close and medium-range defenders
- **Strategy**: Hold angles around objectives, use MP5's range for flexibility

**Flanking**:

- **Best**: Vector or Uzi
- **Why**: Speed and responsiveness for flanking maneuvers
- **Strategy**: Use mobility to get unexpected angles, then push aggressively

## Advanced SMG Techniques

### Slide-Shooting Combo

Many SMG builds benefit from combining tactical slides with shooting:

1. Sprint toward your target
2. Initiate tactical slide
3. Shoot during the slide (maintains accuracy)
4. Slide past cover to unexpected angles

This combo is devastating with the Vector and Uzi, whose fast ADS speeds make slide-shooting incredibly responsive.

### Quick-Scope Transitions

If running an SMG with a 2x scope:

1. ADS with scope for longer-range shots
2. Quickly switch to hip-fire for close-quarters
3. Practice the transition until it's automatic

This technique lets you handle both close and medium ranges effectively.

### Ammo Conservation

SMGs burn through ammo quickly. Conserve with these techniques:

- **Burst fire at range**: Use 3-5 round bursts at 20+ meters to conserve ammo
- **Reload early**: Don't wait for empty magazines — reload during lulls
- **Carry extra ammo**: Always bring more magazines than you think you need
- **Switch weapons**: Use your secondary for very long-range engagements to save SMG ammo
