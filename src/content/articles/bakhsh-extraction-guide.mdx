## Pull-Lever Extraction System Explained

Bakhsh uses a unique **pull-lever extraction system** instead of traditional extraction zones. Here's how it works:

### How Pull-Lever Extractions Work

1. **Find a lever station** — Marked on your map with a gear icon
2. **Pull the lever** — Takes 3-5 seconds (vulnerable)
3. **Wait for extraction vehicle** — Takes 30-60 seconds
4. **Defend the area** — Enemies can hear the lever activation
5. **Board the vehicle** — Extract successfully

### Key Mechanics

| Mechanic | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Lever Pull Time** | 3-5 seconds (interruptible) |
| **Vehicle Arrival** | 30-60 seconds after pull |
| **Sound Radius** | Entire map can hear lever pull |
| **Vehicle Capacity** | 4 players max |
| **Vehicle Duration** | Stays for 30 seconds |
| **Multiple Levers** | Can be pulled simultaneously |

<Callout type="warning" title="Critical Warning">
Pulling a lever alerts EVERYONE on the map. You will be contested. Always assume enemies are coming.
</Callout>

## Bakhsh Pro Tips

### Tip #1: Listen for Lever Pulls
You can hear lever pulls from **anywhere on the map**. Use this to:
- Track enemy extraction plans
- Intercept teams at lever stations
- Time your own lever pulls

**Sound cue:** Distinctive mechanical grinding noise


### Tip #2: Control the Control Tower
The team that holds the Control Tower **controls the match**:
- Best sightlines on the map
- Access to keycard loot
- Central position for rotations
- Rooftop lever station

**Priority target for squads.**


### Tip #3: Use Smoke at Lever Stations
Smoke grenades are **essential** for Bakhsh extractions:
- Cover lever pull animation (3-5 seconds)
- Obscure vehicle arrival area
- Block enemy sightlines during defense

**Carry 2-3 smoke grenades per match.**


### Tip #4: Don't Pull Too Early
Timing is everything:
- **0-8 minutes:** Loot phase, avoid levers
- **8-15 minutes:** Start positioning near lever
- **15-18 minutes:** Pull lever (sweet spot)
- **18-20 minutes:** Too late (may not extract in time)

**Ideal pull time: 14-16 minutes**


### Tip #5: Coordinate with Your Squad
Bakhsh rewards **team coordination**:
- One player pulls lever
- Two players defend angles
- One player watches rear/flanks

**Assign roles before pulling.**
