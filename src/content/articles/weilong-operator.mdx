Currently ranked as **T0** in the meta, Weilong dominates maps with verticality — especially **Dam Zero** and **Bakhsh** — where his jetpack lets him access positions no other operator can reach. His toolkit blends mobility, area denial, and explosive damage in a way that rewards creative, aggressive players who think three-dimensionally.

## Detailed Ability Breakdown

### Passive: Acceleration

Weilong's **Acceleration** passive is a subtle but powerful movement modifier. The longer you sprint in a single direction, the faster you move — building up momentum that makes you increasingly difficult to hit and allows you to cross dangerous areas more quickly.

**Why it matters:**

- **Build momentum before engagements:** Start sprinting well before you reach the fight so you arrive at maximum speed.
- **Cross open areas faster:** When forced to cross exposed terrain, the acceleration passive reduces the time you're vulnerable.
- **Combine with jetpack:** A jetpack dash launched at maximum acceleration speed covers significantly more ground.
- **Hit-and-run tactics:** Use the momentum to dart in, deal damage, and retreat before enemies can track you.

<Callout type="info" title="Pro Tip: Direction Changes Reset Acceleration">
Changing direction significantly resets your acceleration buildup. Plan your routes in straight lines when possible, and use jetpack dashes instead of sharp turns to maintain speed while redirecting.
</Callout>

### Tactical: Jetpack Dash

The **Jetpack Dash** is Weilong's signature ability — a short-duration thruster burst that propels him in any direction. Unlike simple dashes in other games, Weilong's jetpack offers true three-dimensional movement: you can dash forward, backward, sideways, upward, or even diagonally through the air.

**Advanced jetpack techniques:**

- **Vertical flanks:** Use the jetpack to reach rooftops, balconies, and high ground that other operators can't access. This gives you angles nobody expects.
- **Mid-air redirection:** Dash off a ledge, then use your jetpack mid-fall to redirect your trajectory and reach otherwise impossible positions.
- **Escape tool:** When caught in a bad position, jetpack away to create distance or reach safety. The sudden directional change makes you hard to track.
- **Peek assistance:** Use short jetpack bursts to quickly peek around corners or over obstacles while maintaining momentum.
- **Double jump simulation:** Jump, then jetpack immediately after to achieve extreme heights and access elevated sniping positions.

### Ultimate: Compressed Air Cannon

Weilong's **Compressed Air Cannon** is one of the most disruptive ultimates in the game. When deployed, it fires a concentrated blast of pressurized air that **launches enemies into the air**, dealing damage and leaving them completely vulnerable while airborne.

**Why it's so powerful:**

- **Area denial:** Enemies won't push through areas where your cannon is active — the threat of being launched is too significant.
- **Guaranteed damage:** Launched enemies take fall damage and are easy targets while in the air — follow up with gunfire for quick kills.
- **Objective control:** Use it to clear enemies from tight objective areas or chokepoints during pushes.
- **Anti-vehicle support:** While it doesn't destroy vehicles, launching enemy operators out of exposed positions helps your Engineering team deal with them.
- **Disruption:** Even if it doesn't secure a kill, the cannon breaks enemy formations and forces repositioning.

<Callout type="warning" title="Warning: Predictable Launch Arc">
The Compressed Air Cannon has a visible launch arc, giving skilled enemies a brief window to dodge. Aim for chokepoints and confined spaces where escape routes are limited. Combine with C4 to create kill zones where enemies must choose between two bad options.
</Callout>

### Equipment: C4 Charges

Weilong comes equipped with **C4 charges** — remote-detonated explosives that let you set traps, breach fortified positions, or finish off enemies you've launched into the air with your ultimate.

**C4 strategies:**

- **Combo with air cannon:** Place C4 where enemies will be launched into, then detonate for massive damage.
- **Flank traps:** Set C4 on common flanking routes. When enemies try to get behind you, they walk into your trap.
- **Objective denial:** Place C4 near objectives to deter enemy pushes or punish clustered defenders.
- **Building clears:** In Havoc Operations, use C4 to clear rooms before entering — throw it in, detonate, then push through the smoke.

## Best Maps for Weilong

Weilong's jetpack is most effective on maps with significant verticality, multi-story buildings, and elevated positions. While he can be useful anywhere, certain maps let him truly dominate.

### Dam Zero: Vertical Playground

**Dam Zero** is arguably Weilong's best map. The dam structure itself offers massive vertical drops, narrow catwalks, and multiple elevation levels. Weilong can traverse these vertical spaces faster than any other operator, reaching positions that give him complete control over key areas.

**Key Dam Zero strategies:**

- Use the jetpack to reach the top of the dam structure quickly and establish high-ground advantage.
- Control the catwalks by jetpacking between them faster than enemies can track your movement.
- Deploy your Compressed Air Cannon at the base of the dam where enemies funnel through chokepoints.
- Set C4 traps on the most common climbing routes to punish enemies trying to reach your elevated positions.

### Bakhsh: Urban Warfare

**Bakhsh** features dense urban environments with multiple building levels, rooftops, and elevated positions. Weilong excels here because his jetpack lets him access rooftops and upper floors without using stairs or ladders — giving him unexpected angles and escape routes.

**Key Bakhsh strategies:**

- Establish rooftop dominance early. Control the high ground and force enemies to fight on your terms.
- Use jetpack dashes between buildings to flank enemies who think they're safe behind cover.
- Place C4 on stairwells and ladders — the only ways enemies can reach your elevated positions.
- Your Compressed Air Cannon excels in the tight streets where enemies have limited escape routes when launched.

### Other Effective Maps

While Dam Zero and Bakhsh are Weilong's strongest maps, he also performs well on:

- **Multi-story complex maps:** Any map with significant vertical structure lets Weilong access positions others can't reach.
- **Maps with cliffs or elevation changes:** Natural high ground combined with jetpack mobility gives Weilong unmatched control.
- **Maps with multiple approach routes:** Weilong's mobility lets him flank and reposition faster than enemies can adapt.

## Advanced Playstyle Tips

### The Jetpack Ambush

One of Weilong's most devastating tactics is the jetpack ambush. Position yourself on high ground overlooking a common enemy path. When enemies pass below, jetpack down onto them while firing. The combination of unexpected angle, downward momentum, and initial gunfire often secures quick kills before enemies can react.

### Ultimate Economy: Save for Critical Moments

The Compressed Air Cannon has a long charge time, so use it wisely. Save your ultimate for:

- **Objective pushes:** When your team needs to clear a defended position.
- **Counter-flanks:** When enemies try to get behind your team, use the cannon to disrupt them.
- **1vX situations:** When outnumbered, the cannon can separate enemies and create 1v1 scenarios.
- **Finishing damaged enemies:** Use it to launch enemies who are behind cover but low on health.

<Callout type="success" title="Advanced Technique: Jetpack-C4-Air Cannon Combo">
Set C4 in a confined space, then use your air cannon to launch enemies into the C4 blast zone. The combination deals massive damage and is nearly impossible to survive. Practice the timing — detonate just as enemies reach the peak of their launch arc.
</Callout>

### Counter-Play: What to Watch Out For

Weilong is powerful but not invincible. Be aware of these counters:

- **Anti-air operators:** Some operators excel at targeting elevated players. Watch for Recon operators with line of sight to your positions.
- **Engineering class with anti-vehicle weapons:** While you're not a vehicle, the damage output is significant and can punish you for being exposed.
- **Coordinated teams:** Weilong's greatest weakness is being targeted by multiple players simultaneously. Don't overextend without backup.
- **Stun or suppression abilities:** Some operators can disrupt your movement or lock you down. Be ready to jetpack away if you see these abilities coming.

## Tier Ranking: Why Weilong is T0

Weilong consistently earns **T0** status for several compelling reasons:

- **Unique mobility:** No other operator can match his three-dimensional movement options. This alone gives him an advantage in positioning and escape.
- **Map control:** His ability to access high ground quickly lets him control key areas and dictate engagement terms.
- **Disruptive ultimate:** The Compressed Air Cannon is one of the most powerful ultimates for area denial and creating kill opportunities.
- **Versatility:** Effective in both Havoc Operations and All-Out Warfare, making him a valuable pick regardless of mode.
- **Skill expression:** High skill ceiling rewards creative players who master his movement with exceptional results.

## Conclusion

Weilong is the operator for players who think vertically. His jetpack dash opens up an entirely new dimension of tactical movement, while his Compressed Air Cannon ultimate and C4 charges give him unmatched area control. Combined with his acceleration passive, Weilong becomes a force of nature — constantly moving, constantly threatening, and always one step ahead.

To master Weilong, focus on map knowledge — learn where your jetpack can take you, which elevated positions are most valuable, and how to combine your abilities for maximum impact. Practice on Dam Zero and Bakhsh to build muscle memory for your movement, then apply those skills across all maps.

When played well, Weilong doesn't just participate in battles — he reshapes them. He turns the battlefield into his personal playground, forcing enemies to look up, to reconsider their positions, and to respect the vertical space they once ignored. That's why he's T0. That's why he's Weilong.
