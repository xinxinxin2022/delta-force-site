## QJB201 Playstyle — How to Use an LMG

The QJB201 requires a **different approach** than assault rifles. Here's how to maximize effectiveness:

### Positioning is Everything

**Good Positions:**
- Behind cover with clear sightlines
- Elevated positions (rooftops, hills)
- Objective chokepoints
- Prone in grass/bushes

**Bad Positions:**
- Open fields with no cover
- Close-quarters indoor combat
- Flanking routes (too slow)

### The LMG Mindset

1. **Pre-position before fights** — Don't run and gun
2. **Go prone when engaging** — Activates bipod bonus
3. **Sustain fire to suppress** — Force enemies behind cover
4. **Call out targets** — Your suppression enables teammates

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistake">
Never try to flank with the QJB201. You're a walking turret — play like one.
</Callout>

## QJB201 Pro Tips

### Tip #1: Master the Prone Position
The bipod is your best friend. **Always go prone before engaging**. The -40% recoil reduction is game-changing.

### Tip #2: Pre-Fire Common Angles
The QJB201's slow ADS means you should **pre-fire doorways and corners** before enemies appear.

### Tip #3: Communicate with Your Squad
You're a **team weapon**. Call out targets you're suppressing so teammates can flank.

### Tip #4: Reload During Rotations
4-5 second reloads are deadly mid-fight. **Reload every time you move to a new position**.

### Tip #5: Don't Chase Kills
You're slow. If enemies flank you, **fall back to a better position** rather than trying to chase them down.
