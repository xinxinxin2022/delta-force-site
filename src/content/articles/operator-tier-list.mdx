These operators are so strong that they shape team compositions and match outcomes. If you can master any of these, you'll consistently outperform players on lower-tier picks.

<Callout type="info" title="Why These Five Are T0">
All T0 operators share two traits: **versatility** (they work in multiple situations, not just one) and **team impact** (their abilities benefit the whole squad, not just themselves). Uluru and Echo are genuinely good at everything. Red Wolf dominates indoor combat so completely that he's banned in some competitive formats. Mai Xiaowen's intel abilities make her invaluable in any team composition.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Best Beginner T1 Picks">
If you're new to the game, **Hive Medic** and **Gale** are the easiest T1 operators to pick up. Hive Medic keeps your squad alive (teams love healers), and Gale's straightforward aggressive kit is forgiving for newer players. <strong>Luna</strong> is also excellent for beginners who want to learn map control.
</Callout>

## Operator Recommendations by Playstyle

### "I'm a Complete Beginner"

**Pick: Hive Medic ★★★★★**

Hive Medic is the single best operator for new players. Here's why:
- **Auto-healing** keeps you alive while you learn the game
- **Smoke cover** gives you an escape tool when things go wrong
- **Fast revive (1.4s)** makes you invaluable to any squad
- **Teams always want a healer** — you'll never struggle to find a group

Second choices: **Gale** (straightforward aggressive kit) or **Luna** (learn map control safely).

### "I Like Aggressive, In-Your-Face Combat"

**Pick: Red Wolf ★★★★★**

The undisputed king of aggressive play. Tactical Slide for repositioning, Breach Smoke for intel, Exoskeleton for clutch plays. If you like to be in the action, nothing comes close.

Second choice: **Gale** for indoor aggression, or **Terry Mosa** for raw assault power.

### "I Prefer Playing Smart and Tactical"

**Pick: Mai Xiaowen ★★★★★**

The best information operator in the game. Mai Xiaowen's abilities let you control the flow of a match by knowing where enemies are before they know where you are. Perfect for players who win with knowledge rather than raw gunskill.

Second choice: **Silver Wing** for drone-based recon, or **Echo** for all-round tactical play.

### "I Want to Support My Team"

**Pick: Hive Medic or Weilong**

Hive Medic keeps everyone alive. Weilong provides unique air support and drone reconnaissance. Both are highly valued in squad play.

### "I Play Solo Queue"

**Pick: Hacker Claw or Red Wolf**

Solo queue rewards self-sufficient operators who can carry fights alone. Hacker Claw excels at aggressive solo plays, and Red Wolf's kit lets you create your own opportunities without relying on teammates.

## Operator Ability Tips &amp; Hidden Mechanics

Here are some lesser-known tips that can give you an edge:

<Callout type="tip" title="Red Wolf Slide on Slopes">
Red Wolf's Tactical Slide travels **significantly further on slopes and ramps**. Use this to cover open areas quickly — a slide down a hill can carry you 2-3x the normal distance.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Luna's Ultimate Limitation">
Luna's ultimate ability **cannot see through smoke**. If enemies are inside a smoke cloud, Luna's recon won't reveal them. Plan accordingly when pushing smoked positions.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Hive Medic 1.4s Revive Trick">
Hive Medic's base revive speed is already the fastest in the game at 1.4 seconds. With the right attachments and the Hormone Gun ability, you can revive teammates before enemies can finish reloading. Practice the timing — it's a game-changer.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Mai Xiaowen Positioning">
Mai Xiaowen is at her strongest when **holding positions and controlling retreats**. Don't push forward with her — let your assault operators go first, then use Mai's abilities to track and control the engagement from a safe position.
</Callout>

### What is the best operator for beginners in Delta Force?
**Hive Medic** is the best operator for beginners. She has auto-healing, smoke cover, fast revives (1.4s), and every squad needs a healer. You'll always find teams willing to play with you.

### Who is the strongest operator in Delta Force 2026?
**Uluru** is widely considered the strongest overall operator (T0 ceiling), with **Echo** as the best all-round alternative. For aggressive play, **Red Wolf** is unmatched.

There are **15 operators** in the current roster, spanning Assault, Recon, Support, Engineer, and Specialist roles.

### Is Red Wolf still good in Season 10?
Yes. Red Wolf remains T0 for indoor combat and aggressive play. His Tactical Slide + Breach Smoke + Exoskeleton combo is still the most powerful offensive kit in the game.

**Nameless** and **Shepherd** are the weakest operators in the current meta. Shepherd has only a 10% recommendation rate, and Nameless offers low impact on match outcomes.
